The sound files in this folder (<theme>-loop.ogg and <theme>-hit.ogg) are synthesized from scratch
by this module's own script (tools/make-sounds.py) out of noise, sine waves, filters and envelopes.
No recordings and no third-party samples went into them. They are dedicated to the public domain
under Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0):
